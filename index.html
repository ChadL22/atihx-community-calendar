Built by our communities

ATIHx gatherings are independently organized by local community members who want to meet in person and talk about building a better tech future.

The Philadelphia and Washington, D.C. communities built this calendar to make our gatherings easier to find in one place. It is a community project, not an official All Tech Is Human site. For ATIH's own programs and events, visit alltechishuman.org.

Between gatherings, the conversation continues in ATIH's Slack community. Already a member? Sign in to Slack.
