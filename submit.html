How it works

  • A community lead checks each submission before it goes on the calendar, usually within a day or two.
  • We check for duplicates automatically, so if someone already added your event we'll let you know.
  • Once it's live, it shows up for everyone subscribed to the calendar.
  • Need to change something after submitting? Email your community lead.
