Add An Event

Events are submitted to All Tech Is Human's calendar on Luma. Once it's accepted there, a DMV or Philadelphia event appears here automatically, usually within an hour.

Where does your event live?

1

A new Luma event

Haven't created it anywhere yet? Create it on Luma and it's submitted to the All Tech Is Human calendar for you.

  1. Sign in to Luma and fill in your event details.
  2. Publish. It goes to the calendar admins for review.
Create on Luma ↗
2

An existing Luma event

Already on Luma, under your own account or another calendar? Submit it to ours.

  1. Open the All Tech Is Human calendar on Luma.
  2. Click Submit Event, then choose:
＋ Create New Event ✦ Submit Existing Luma Event 🔗 Submit External Event
  1. Pick your event from the list.
Open the ATIH calendar ↗
3

A non-Luma event

On Eventbrite, Meetup, a university or organization page? You can still submit it through Luma.

  1. Open the All Tech Is Human calendar on Luma.
  2. Click Submit Event, then choose:
＋ Create New Event ✦ Submit Existing Luma Event 🔗 Submit External Event
  1. Paste the event's link and add the date, time and location.
Open the ATIH calendar ↗

Location matters. Only events held in person in the DMV (DC, suburban Maryland and Northern Virginia) or the Philadelphia area appear on this calendar. Online events are not included.

Rather not use Luma? Share your event in your community's channel in ATIH's Slack and a community lead can add it.
